VIDEO DOWNLOADER by Tanvir  (Windows)
=====================================

A free app that saves videos, photos and music from YouTube, Instagram,
Pinterest, Facebook, TikTok, X, SoundCloud and about 1,000 other sites.

বাংলা নির্দেশনা নিচে আছে।


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- Internet on the first launch. The app sets itself up once (1 to 2 minutes).


INSTALL (only once)
-------------------
1. Right-click the zip file > Extract All > Extract.
   Don't run anything from inside the zip.
2. Move the "Video Downloader" folder wherever you like, for example
   Documents. Keep everything inside it together.
3. Double-click "Start Video Downloader".
   If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info,
   then Run anyway. This happens because the app is new, not because
   anything is wrong.
4. The first time, a black window sets things up for 1 to 2 minutes.
   Then the app opens in your web browser.

Keep the black window open while you use the app. Close it to quit.

Shortcut on the Desktop: right-click "Start Video Downloader" >
Show more options > Send to > Desktop (create shortcut).


HOW TO USE
----------
1. Copy a link from YouTube, Instagram, Pinterest or any other site.
2. Paste it into the app (Ctrl+V, or press "Paste from clipboard").
   You can paste several links, one per line.
3. Choose how to save it:
     Editing MP4   H.264 video that opens in Premiere Pro, After Effects, CapCut
     Max quality   the sharpest version, up to 4K or 8K
     Audio         FLAC, MP3 320 kbps, or Original M4A (the site's own audio, untouched)
   Photo links are recognised by themselves and saved as the original images.
4. Press Download. When it finishes, press "Show file".

Your files go to:  Downloads\Video Downloader  (one folder for each site)
Press "Help" at the top of the app for quick tips.


PRIVATE POSTS, INSTAGRAM AND AGE-RESTRICTED VIDEOS
--------------------------------------------------
Use Firefox for this. Windows does not let other apps read Chrome or Edge
logins.
1. Log in to the site in Firefox.
2. In the app, open the "No browser login" menu and pick
   "Use my Firefox login".


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
When a download fails, the app writes what to do right under it.
For anything else:

Your antivirus removed or blocked "uv.exe"
   Restore it or allow it. It is uv, the official open-source Python tool
   from Astral, which the app uses to set itself up.

"Setup did not finish"
   Check your internet and run "Start Video Downloader" again. If it still
   fails, paste this into the File Explorer address bar and press Enter:
       %LOCALAPPDATA%\Video Downloader
   delete everything in that folder, then run the app again.

The browser didn't open
   Open your browser yourself and go to:  http://127.0.0.1:8765

Nothing happens when you double-click
   Make sure you extracted the zip first (step 1).

"The site refused the download" (YouTube 403) or "limiting downloads"
   Wait a few minutes and try again. The app updates its download engine
   every day, which fixes most of these. A Firefox login also helps.

The page says it can't reach the app
   The black window was closed. Run "Start Video Downloader" again.


UNINSTALL
---------
Delete the "Video Downloader" folder, then delete
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Video Downloader  (paste it into the File Explorer address bar).


PLEASE USE IT FAIRLY
--------------------
Save videos, photos and music you made, own, or have permission to use.
Re-uploading other people's work can get your account a copyright strike.
DRM-protected services (Netflix, Spotify, Apple Music) can't be downloaded.
